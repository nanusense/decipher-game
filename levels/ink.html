INVISIBLE INK

TO WHOEVER FINDS THIS
I could not say it plainly, so I hid it here —
between these ordinary words, in plain sight.
Not every word means what it seems.
Read all of it. Every line. Every word.
Once you find it,
with confidence. You will know it when you see it.

Drag the light across every word.
— A friend

Not all ink is equal.
